# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

中文：我们致力于提供一个不受骚扰、尊重不同经验与背景的协作环境。讨论可以尖锐，不能
针对个人。技术批评应具体、可验证，并以改进项目为目标。

English: We commit to a harassment-free, respectful collaboration space for people of
different backgrounds and experience levels. Discussion may be direct, but never personal.
Technical criticism should be specific, verifiable, and aimed at improving the project.

## 可接受行为 / Expected Behavior

- 尊重地沟通，善意解释分歧 / Communicate respectfully and explain disagreements in good faith.
- 聚焦代码、产品行为和证据 / Focus on code, product behavior, and evidence.
- 接受纠正并承担错误 / Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- 对账号、隐私和安全信息保持克制 / Handle account, privacy, and security information carefully.

## 不可接受行为 / Unacceptable Behavior

- 骚扰、威胁、歧视、侮辱或持续的人身攻击 / Harassment, threats, discrimination, insults,
  or sustained personal attacks.
- 未经允许公开他人的身份、账号、地址或私人通信 / Publishing another person's identity,
  accounts, address, or private communications without permission.
- 在 Issue、PR 或日志中发布真实凭据或私人下载链接 / Posting real credentials or private
  download URLs in issues, pull requests, or logs.
- 垃圾信息、恶意破坏或故意绕过项目安全边界 / Spam, sabotage, or deliberate bypass of
  project security boundaries.

## 执行 / Enforcement

维护者可编辑或删除内容、锁定讨论、拒绝贡献，或临时/永久限制参与。严重或重复违规会采取
更强措施。维护者也必须遵守本准则。

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Severe or repeated violations receive
stronger action. Maintainers are also bound by this code.

如需私密报告行为问题，可使用
[GitHub 私密报告](https://github.com/soluna/zlib-anna-skill/security/advisories/new)，标题注明
`Conduct report`；不要在公开 Issue 中放入敏感细节。

For a confidential conduct report, use
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/soluna/zlib-anna-skill/security/advisories/new) with
`Conduct report` in the title. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue.
